Oathe is an exclusive advisory firm for ultra-high-net-worth individuals, professional athletes, and founders.

Oathe bridges the worlds of professional sports, private wealth, and brand strategy. Founded by an insider with deep relationships across leagues and investment circles, Oathe operates as part investment scout, part lifestyle concierge, and part brand architect — with a singular philosophy: “We only win if you win.”

(Approach)

The client needed a digital presence that reflected their unique hybrid model — not a typical sports agency, not a financial advisor, but something in between.

The challenge was to communicate credibility and exclusivity while keeping the brand intentionally understated.

The website had to attract two very different audiences — athletes and founders — without diluting the core identity.

(Challenge)

We started with a strategic foundation — defining key messaging and tone of voice.

The design direction emphasized structure, confidence, and restraint — reflecting the firm’s analytical nature.

Every interaction and visual element was designed to feel deliberate, creating a sense of calm authority.

“The team instantly understood what we were trying to build — something that felt private, elevated, and unlike anything in the sports or investment space.
 They translated that into a digital presence that feels exactly right — confident, minimal, and quietly powerful.”
John Carter
Founder & Managing Partner, Oathe
(Outcome)

The new Oathe Group website became more than just a redesign — it redefined how the brand communicates trust and expertise in a competitive industry.

By focusing on structure, clarity, and tone, we created a digital experience that feels effortless yet deeply strategic.

The design now mirrors the firm's values: precision, stability, and long-term vision.
